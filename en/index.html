01 · Scope

PingDuo (Chinese name: 乒乓拍档) is an iPhone/iPad table-tennis video analysis app developed by Weiye. Its application identifier is com.weiye.pingduo.

This policy applies to the current local-feature TestFlight beta. No sign-in or subscription is required, and analysis is unlimited. Account, subscription, remote quota, and application-server diagnostic services are not enabled in this round.

Before introducing accounts, paid subscriptions, cloud services, or new data processing, we will update this policy and the relevant app information.

02 · Local videos and data

Videos you select are copied to app-managed local storage for decoding, on-device Core ML analysis, rally playback, and export. The app does not automatically scan or import your entire photo library.

To provide these features, the app stores the following on your device:

  • Copies of selected videos, thumbnails, analysis checkpoints, and results.
  • Video titles and dates, opponent tags you enter, rally boundaries, favorites, and exclusion settings.
  • Timing and display preferences, and viewing/exposure history used to order your local rally feed.
  • Local error codes, versions, and task states used to diagnose problems.

The current beta does not upload source videos, video frames, analysis results, opponent tags, or these local records to the developer’s application server. It has no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDK, and does not use this information for tracking across apps.

03 · System permissions

Permission or entry pointPurpose and choice
System photo pickerYou select the videos to import. The app receives only your selected content.
Photo library accessUsed to read the selected video’s original title and capture date. Import remains available if you decline; information available in the file is used instead.
Adding to PhotosUsed when you export a rally or highlight movie to your photo library.

You can change permissions in iOS/iPadOS Settings. Declining permission to add to Photos affects saving exported movies to Photos. The current version does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location access.

04 · TestFlight and feedback

Apple provides TestFlight. During testing, Apple collects installation and usage information, device and operating-system information, and crash logs, and makes test information available to the developer. With email invitations, the developer can also see your invitation email address, name (if supplied), and invitation status.

When you submit feedback, Apple shares your comments, screenshots, and related diagnostic information with the developer. Screenshots may contain video frames, titles, or other personal information. Review their content before sending, and avoid including information you do not want to share.

The developer uses test information only to respond to feedback, diagnose problems, and improve PingDuo. We do not sell it or share TestFlight test information with third parties. The app’s own diagnostic records remain local in this beta and are not sent to an application server.

Apple’s collection and retention are governed by its policies. Read Apple’s TestFlight & Privacy information.

05 · Retention and deletion

Local project data remains until you delete the project or remove the app. Deleting a video project removes its app-managed video copy and related analysis data, without deleting the original video in Photos.

Opponent tags and global preferences may remain after a project is deleted. Exported movies are managed independently in Photos and must be deleted there. Uninstalling the app removes its sandbox data; depending on your device settings, system backups or restores may retain or restore some app data.

The developer uses feedback only for as long as needed to address feedback and improve the software. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information under the developer’s control. Apple-managed TestFlight data is subject to Apple’s retention and deletion rules.

06 · Website and third parties

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no added advertisements, analytics, tracking scripts, or data-entry forms. As the hosting provider, GitHub may process IP addresses, request logs, and other information needed to serve this website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

On-device storage and system backups depend on your Apple device settings. Apple TestFlight, system Photos, and GitHub services are governed by their respective policies.
